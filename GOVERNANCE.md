# Governance

This project currently uses a maintainer-led, consensus-seeking model appropriate for an early-stage proposal.

## Roles

- **Contributors** participate through issues, discussions, documentation, code, data, or reviews.
- **Maintainers** review contributions, steward releases, enforce community standards, and protect methodological and schema coherence.

The initial repository owner acts as the initial maintainer. Additional maintainers may be invited based on sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated stewardship.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through pull-request review. Material changes to terminology, equations, schemas, governance, privacy expectations, or compatibility should begin as a public proposal issue. Maintainers seek rough consensus, document major objections, and make the final decision when consensus is not possible.

No proposal becomes scientifically established merely because it is merged. Evidence status must remain explicit.

## Releases and compatibility

Before 1.0, breaking changes are expected and must be documented. Releases should identify the versions of the framework, schemas, evaluation protocol, and REEF implementation that they contain.

## Conflicts of interest

Participants should disclose financial, organizational, or research interests that could reasonably affect a proposal. Maintainers may ask a conflicted reviewer to recuse from a final decision.

## Conduct and security

Conduct concerns follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
