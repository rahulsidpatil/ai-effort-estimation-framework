# Telemetry and Observation

## Principles

Collect the minimum data needed to evaluate estimates. Prefer team- and activity-level measurements over individual surveillance. Participation and collection must follow applicable law, policy, consent, and data-governance requirements.

## Observation record

An observation should identify the project; estimate, scenario, schema, and model versions; measurement window; actual PH by work package, activity, and role; elapsed time; realized organizational friction; delivery-mode and AI-use facts; scope and context changes; cost or commercial outcomes when legitimately in scope; exclusions; data-collection method; and quality notes.

## Measurement rules

- Record PH, not story points or elapsed hours, as actual effort.
- Do not infer PH from calendar duration without a documented conversion and uncertainty.
- Record wait time, capacity loss, and human effort separately.
- Separate gross AI benefit hypotheses from observed AI-induced preparation, review, verification, correction, governance, and recovery effort.
- Keep original observations immutable; corrections create a new version with provenance.
- Separate original scope from approved scope changes.
- Preserve estimate-time context and record later changes in tools, models, practices, team composition, policy, and environments.
- Record missingness and confidence rather than inventing precision.
- Avoid individual names and raw prompts unless essential, consented, and appropriately protected.

## Publication

Only synthetic, anonymized, or explicitly licensed data suitable for public redistribution belongs in this repository. De-identification is contextual and must include a re-identification-risk review.
