Overview Overview

REEF product experience

Understand an estimate before committing to it.

REEF helps delivery teams produce explainable software-effort estimates when AI assistance, uncertainty, and organizational constraints affect the work.

Interactive product preview

The workflow and interactions demonstrate the intended REEF experience. All project data and calculated values are illustrative. This tour does not calculate or save a real estimate.

What REEF does

Turns a delivery brief and evidence into a decision you can inspect.

01

Structure the work

Make scope, boundaries, dependencies, and missing information visible.

02

Explain the range

Show likely and higher-confidence effort with the uncertainty behind both.

03

Compare approaches

Separate AI benefits, AI overhead, organizational effects, and delivery scenarios.

04

Support review

Keep assumptions, evidence, warnings, and commercial treatment traceable.

05

Learn from delivery

Compare the estimate with actual delivery without scoring individual people.

What this tour demonstrates

Follow one fictional project from brief to learning.

You will see how an estimation lead, delivery contributors, reviewers, and a commercial lead use the same traceable estimate through ten stages.

By the end, you will understand

  • what information REEF needs and why;
  • how effort ranges and confidence are explained;
  • how AI benefits, costs, and uncertainty stay visible;
  • how review and commercial views use one baseline; and
  • how actual delivery can improve future estimates.
